Safety & Privacy

Threat-aware usage guidance for organizers and support teams.

Operational safety basics

  • Do not post detailed plans publicly.
  • Use role-based communication and need-to-know sharing.
  • Prepare legal + medical escalation contacts in advance.

Data hygiene basics

  • Avoid entering names, IDs, or sensitive personal details.
  • Prefer coarse location planning where possible.
  • Treat outputs as advisory and time-sensitive.

Privacy position

Basic risk checks are designed to work without account creation. Keep your operational security posture stronger than your tooling assumptions.
