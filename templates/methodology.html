Methodology (Plain Language)

Transparent enough for trust. Simple enough to use under pressure.

Scoring inputs

  • Recent incident frequency and trajectory
  • Historical intensity patterns by location
  • Detention/facility-adjacent signal factors
  • Contextual weighting from oversight-linked records

Confidence and limits

  • Confidence is higher where coverage is denser and more recent.
  • Low-data areas may understate or overstate true conditions.
  • Rapid policy and operational shifts can change risk faster than datasets update.

Always combine this score with local observer reports and legal support context.
