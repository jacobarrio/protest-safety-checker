For Organizers

Use this as a planning aid, not a single source of truth.

Best use cases

  • Pre-event route and time selection
  • Role assignment (de-escalation, legal observer, medics)
  • Go / no-go and contingency planning

7-step pre-action checklist

  • Run risk check for target city + nearby fallback area.
  • Cross-check with local trusted on-the-ground intel.
  • Set legal hotline + emergency contact protocol.
  • Share de-arrest and de-escalation plans.
  • Define regroup points and exit routes.
  • Limit sensitive data exposure in chat and docs.
  • Brief team on changing conditions trigger points.
