How to read this page

Three confidence tags so you can judge reliability:

Label
Meaning
Verified
Direct record (official contract/award text) clearly supports the claim.
Partial
Spending record is real, but facility/program mapping is not fully explicit.
Inference
Pattern-based signal only — useful lead, not proof.
Quick rule: Use Verified for strong claims, treat Partial as provisional, treat Inference as a lead to investigate.
