How to read this page (plain English)
We use three confidence tags so you can judge reliability in seconds:
LabelWhat it means
VerifiedWe found a direct record (official contract/award text) that clearly supports the claim.
PartialThe spending record is real, but the exact facility/program mapping is not fully explicit yet.
InferencePattern-based signal only (useful lead, not proof).
Quick rule: use Verified for strong claims, treat Partial as provisional, and treat Inference as a lead to investigate.
